The Honest Story Behind This Blog

I didn't start Vibe with Amit because I had it all figured out. I started it because I didn't — and I wanted somewhere to put it all down.

By profession, I'm a systems engineer. I understand complex technical environments, how to solve problems methodically, how to think in systems. But when I started exploring AI and vibe coding in my spare time, I quickly realised something: even with a technical background, this space moves so fast it can feel overwhelming. There's always a new tool, a new model, a new way of building things.

"I thought — if I'm finding this hard to navigate, what's it like for someone with no technical background at all?"

That question is what turned a personal habit into a public blog. Because the truth is, you don't need to be an engineer to use AI. You don't need to understand how any of it works under the hood. You just need someone to break it down clearly, show you what's actually worth your time, and walk you through it step by step.

That's what I'm trying to be. Not a guru. Not an influencer. Just someone a few steps ahead on the same path — turning around to share what I've learned.

Every article on this blog comes from something I've genuinely explored, tested, or built myself. If I write about a tool, I've used it. If I walk you through building something, I've built it. Nothing here is theoretical. It's all real, documented, and written for humans — not algorithms.
